📊 Retail Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard
🧾 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive retail sales analysis using Power BI, focusing on revenue performance, customer behavior, product category insights, and seasonal sales trends.

The goal of the analysis is to transform raw retail transaction data into actionable business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboarding)
Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning & Preparation)
Data Analysis & Business Intelligence Techniques
📈 Executive Summary

The retail business generated 456,000 in total sales revenue across 1,000 customer transactions, reflecting a stable and diversified revenue structure across Electronics, Clothing, and Beauty categories.

While all categories performed relatively closely, Electronics emerged as the top-performing segment, slightly outperforming Clothing and Beauty in total revenue contribution.

Customer analysis revealed that individuals aged 36 years and above represent the most valuable segment, contributing the majority of total revenue.

Additionally, sales performance demonstrated strong seasonal fluctuations, with peaks observed in May, October, and December, indicating that consumer demand is heavily influenced by holidays and promotional cycles.
